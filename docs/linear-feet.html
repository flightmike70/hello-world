Workbook rules & calculation method

Automatic stacking: up to 54 in tall and 2,500 lb per piece. Not stackable or heavier pieces use one-high. A maximum-stack entry can override the automatic height cutoff, limited by trailer height.

Workbook LF = (quantity ÷ stack high) × length in inches ÷ (12 × whole pieces across). Add all lines, then divide by the safety factor. Fractional floor positions are preserved to match the workbook.

Whole-row planning space rounds stacks and rows up separately for each line. It compares both floor orientations per line, choosing minimum whole-row space, then minimum theoretical LF. It does not combine different lines. It is shown before the safety factor.

Effective cube uses 100 in height for non-stackable, overweight or over-54-in freight unless the workbook permits stacking. This is not actual cargo volume.

Limits & source

Source: Sterling Linear Foot Cost Calculator.xlsx, “Quote Calculator”, I18:N117 and B11/E11/H11/K11. Formula version: sterling-lf-54-v2. The older 48-in / 101-in workbook is not used.

Loading sequence, crush limits, tie-downs, axle distribution, weight per stack, door openings, carrier rules and mixed-footprint packing require carrier review. Length and width may swap automatically; height and pricing remain unchanged. Invalid or oversized pieces stop the calculation rather than inheriting spreadsheet error-to-zero behavior.
