Dock Crew Cheat Sheet: Avoiding W&I Re-Class Fees

  • 1. Always Measure Extreme Points: Carrier overhead laser dimensioners scan from the floor to the highest carton corner and outermost overhang edge.
  • 2. Eliminate Pallet Overhang: A 2-inch overhang on a 48×40" skid turns the billing footprint into 52×44", adding 19% more cubic volume and dropping PCF.
  • 3. Include Skid Tare Weight: Always weigh the shipment on the pallet. Adding 40–50 lbs of wood pallet weight increases density and can keep a borderline load in a lower class.
  • 4. Write Both NMFC & Sub-Item: On density-tiered items (like 156600 or 114000), omitting the Sub number triggers automatic carrier inspection audits.
  • 5. Declare KD (Knocked Down) When Flat: Disassembled furniture or shelving packed flat qualifies for Class 70–85 instead of Class 150–250 when Set Up (SU).

Key Formulas Used

Volume (ft³) = (L × W × H) ÷ 1,728
Density (PCF) = Weight (lbs) ÷ Volume (ft³)

1 cubic foot = 1,728 cubic inches (12" × 12" × 12").
